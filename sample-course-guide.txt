1. TRANSITIONING FROM SS3 TO UNIVERSITY LIFE
- Unlike secondary school where timetables are fixed in one classroom, university study relies on
  credit units, lecture halls, tutorials, continuous assessments (CAs), and independent reading.
- Always check your course syllabus, credit unit load, and examination dates early in the semester.

2. UNDERSTANDING CREDIT UNITS
- Each university course carries a credit unit value based on lecture and tutorial hours.
- Completing coursework and passing assessments earns the credit units toward your semester total.

3. STUDY & EXAM READINESS TIPS
- Review topics weekly rather than cramming before exams.
- Practice past-style questions under timed conditions and check every answer against core concepts.
